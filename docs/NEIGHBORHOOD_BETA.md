# TailTots Neighborhood Beta

## Cheapest Launch Path

Use TailTots as a private PWA first. Families open one link, try it on phones and iPads, and can add it to their home screen without waiting for app-store approval.

## Week 1 Goal

Invite 5 to 10 trusted families who have pets or kids interested in helping with pet care.

## Parent Invite Message

Hi neighbors, I am testing TailTots, a family pet-care app that helps kids learn responsibility, kindness, money choices, and pet-care routines. Parents control profiles, approvals, rewards, and which neighborhood helper jobs kids can see.

We are starting with a small neighborhood beta. If you want to try it with your family, open this link:

`PASTE_LIVE_LINK_HERE`

Best on phone, iPad, tablet, or kitchen-counter display. Feedback is welcome.

## Beta Rules

- Start invite-only.
- Use parent-controlled job visibility.
- Do not share child contact information.
- Ask every beta parent for one usability issue and one feature they would actually use weekly.

## What To Measure

- Number of families who open the link.
- Number of kids who complete a mission.
- Number of parents who approve a task.
- Whether parents understand job visibility.
- Whether kids can switch profiles without help.

## Store Decision

Move to Google Play or Amazon only after at least 10 families try the PWA and 3 families use it more than once.
