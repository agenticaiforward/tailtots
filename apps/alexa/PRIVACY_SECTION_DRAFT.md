## Voice features (Alexa)

**What the skill is.** "TailTots for Parents" is a parent-facing Alexa skill.
It is not directed to children and contains no advertising. To use it, a
parent links their existing TailTots parent account through the Alexa app
using the same sign-in they use on tailtots.com.

**What data it uses.** Once linked, the skill reads only the minimum family
data needed to answer the parent's questions: children's display names,
mission titles and completion states, pending parent approvals, Kid Bank
balances, and streak counts. It does not collect children's voice recordings,
does not transcribe children's speech, and never shows advertising to
children.

**How data flows.** When you link your account, TailTots issues the skill an
access token bound to your parent account. The token can only read your
family's summary through TailTots' own servers — no family data is sent to
Amazon, and no raw child data is shared with advertisers or third-party
sponsors. The authorization behind the token is your verified parental
consent (the same sign-in and consent you gave on tailtots.com, including
verifiable parental consent where COPPA requires it).

**Retention and deletion.** Voice requests are processed to answer you and
are not used to build profiles of your child. Deleting your TailTots account
or unlinking the skill in the Alexa app immediately stops all data access;
you can also request full deletion of your family's data at any time via
the process described in this policy.

**Kids category.** This skill is published as a general-audience parent tool,
not a Kids-category skill, and it must not be used by children on their own.
